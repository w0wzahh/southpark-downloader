# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Critique code and ideas without attacking people.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming to people with different experience levels.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Assume good faith when something is unclear.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project usable for everyone.
